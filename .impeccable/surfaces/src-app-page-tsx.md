# Landing · Networking Night

Scope: public landing (`/`) and the purchase flow that starts from it. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.
Audience: BNI members (Pioneros, Metamorfosis, other chapters) and their guests, arriving by word of mouth and WhatsApp links, mostly on phones. Job: understand the event in seconds and buy tickets (1–10, a name per guest).
Constraints: Spanish only; BNI red stays recognizable; never mention Metamorfosis' age; no photos yet; no invented facts.
Unresolved: agenda, closing time, "novios"/padrinos as people, gift-table copy, surprises.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The event is a ticket to a black-tie wedding drawn in the Vienna Secession manner; the page refuses the floral cream invitation and the corporate BNI event page alike.
OWN-WORLD: Gallery-white ground (#F6F5F1) on a warm grey page (#E9E7E1), lacquer-black hairlines and double rules, strict symmetry, Forum roman capitals widely tracked, Josefin Sans labels; BNI red (#C8102E) only on the active thing (buy action, ampersand, one ring). Every framed surface is a ticket: concave corners, double rule, ring medallion, perforated tear line with side notches. Motifs: P&M monogram seal, interlocked rings (black + red). No square icons, no perforated square bands.
STORY: Visitors understand Pioneros (first BNI chapter in Mexico) and Metamorfosis (the newest in León) are "marrying" at Networking Night; believe it is elegant, fun and worth $600 (dinner, toast, DJ, bar, valet); act by tearing off the stub: buying.
